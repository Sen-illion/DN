\section{Narrative and Text Quality Evaluation}
\label{sec:narrative-quality}

This section is reserved for narrative and text-quality evaluation. Planned metrics include worldview coherence, option actionability, plot continuity, state consistency, and human or model-judge ratings. These experiments will determine whether \dn's textual and structured narrative outputs remain coherent and playable beyond the latency measurements reported in Section~\ref{sec:efficiency}.
