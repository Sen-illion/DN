\section{Visual Quality Evaluation}
\label{sec:visual-quality}

This section is reserved for the visual quality experiments. It will evaluate whether generated images are visually faithful to the benchmark theme, consistent across turns, and free from obvious artifacts. Planned metrics include visual fidelity, theme alignment, character or scene consistency, next-turn visual continuity, and artifact rate. This section complements Section~\ref{sec:efficiency}: the efficiency evaluation measures how quickly content is delivered, while the visual quality evaluation will measure whether the delivered images are useful for narrative play.
